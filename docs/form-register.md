## Verified Facts

### SGLI maximum coverage
- **$500,000**, in $50,000 increments — current maximum.
- **Increased from $400,000 to $500,000, effective March 1, 2023.** Exact VA language: *"The maximum amount of SGLI coverage will be increased from $400,000 to $500,000"* … *"The increased maximum coverage amount of $500,000 will become effective March 1, 2023."*
- Sources: https://www.va.gov/life-insurance/options-eligibility/sgli/ · https://www.benefits.va.gov/INSURANCE/SGLI-increase-faqs.asp
- No change more recent than March 2023 was found on VA's own pages.

### PADD (Person Authorized to Direct Disposition)
Official definition (War Department / Military OneSource, *Know Your PADD Rights*):

> "A PADD is someone designated by a service member on their **DD Form 93, 'Record of Emergency Data,'** or designated by law if a service member did not designate a person to make decisions regarding the preparation and disposition of their human remains."

- **Recorded on: DD Form 93.**
- PADD duties include directing preparation/transportation of remains, selecting the funeral home, casket/urn, viewing the remains, and directing final disposition; PADD may seek reimbursement up to the service's maximum allowance and receives round-trip transportation to the service.
- If the PADD is not already the next of kin eligible for a burial flag, the PADD receives an **interment flag** presented by the honor guard.
- Related but distinct: **DD Form 3045, "Statement of Disposition of Military Remains"** exists and is a separate document — its exact role was **not** verified here.
- Source: https://download.militaryonesource.mil/12038/Casualty/padd-rights-trifold.pdf

### NPRC turnaround for a replacement DD 214
**No official committed turnaround time exists.** NARA explicitly declines to give one:

> "Response times from the National Personnel Records Center (NPRC) vary depending on the complexity of your request, the availability of the records, and our workload."

Concrete figures NARA *does* publish:
- NPRC receives roughly **4,000–5,000 requests per day**.
- *"Allow about 10 days for us to receive and initiate processing your request, then you may check the status of your request."*
- *"Please do not send a follow-up request before 90 days have elapsed, as it may cause further delays."*
- **Emergency/funeral route:** mark the request "Emergency Request" in eVetRecs (or the "Purpose" block of the SF 180) and fax to the Customer Service Team at **(314) 801-0764**. For interment at a VA national cemetery, contact the **National Cemetery Scheduling Office at 800-535-1117** — NARA works directly with VA staff to verify service.
- Sources: https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records · https://www.archives.gov/personnel-records-center/military-personnel · https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records/emergencies

**Any specific "X weeks typical" number is UNVERIFIED — do not publish one.**

### Digital routes to a DD 214
- **eVetRecs (National Archives)** — the online request tool, reached from VA.gov. Requires an **identity-verified ID.me** account. This is the primary digital route, and it is NARA's system, not VA's.
- **milConnect (DoD)** — provides service records for members discharged after:
  - Air Force — after **October 1, 2004**
  - Army — after **October 1, 2002**
  - Marine Corps — after **January 1, 1999**
  - Navy — after **January 1, 1995**
  Requires a milConnect account.
- **eBenefits — DEPRECATED.** The portal has sunset; functions moved to VA.gov. VA's current DD 214 page makes no mention of it. Do not route anyone to eBenefits.
- Sources: https://www.va.gov/records/get-military-service-records/ · https://www.ebenefits.va.gov/ebenefits/manage/documents

## Notes, Corrections and Caveats

**Note 1 — DD 214 title vs. DoDI title.** Searching surfaces DoD Instruction 1336.01, *"Certificate of Uniformed Service (DD Form 214/5 Series)."* That is the title of the **governing instruction**, not the title of the form. Three official sources (VA, NCA, NARA) all give the form's title as **"Certificate of Release or Discharge from Active Duty."** Use that. Separately, Military OneSource's own flyer colloquially calls it a *"Report of Separation"* — that is a nickname, not the official title.

**Note 2 — 21P-530EZ full title.** The complete official title carries the statutory citation: *"Application for Burial Benefits (Under 38 U.S.C. Chapter 23)."* Shortening it to "Application for Burial Benefits" is accurate but incomplete.

**Note 3 — DD 2656-2 evidence quality.** This PDF is an XFA/LiveCycle form whose body would not render to text. The title **"Survivor Benefit Plan (SBP) Termination Request"** is confirmed by the document titles published on three official .mil hosts (esd.whs.mil, home.army.mil, arpc.afrc.af.mil), and ARPC labels its copy *"DD Form 2656-2, SBP Termination Request, April 2009."* Consistent across all three, but not read off the rendered form face like 2656-1/-6/-7 were.

**Note 4 — SGLV 8286 is no longer the default path.** VA now directs full-time SGLI members to the **SGLI Online Enrollment System (SOES)** via milConnect ("Manage my SGLI"). SGLV 8286 remains current for members with **part-time** SGLI coverage. A NOK-facing product should not tell an active-duty member to file paper 8286 by default.

**Note 5 — DD 2656 edition drift.** The copy read (home.army.mil) is the **OCT 2018** edition. A Marine Corps copy is indexed as **MARCH 2022**, which appears to be the current edition. Confirm the edition before embedding a page/field map. The SBP election is on the form either way — the Principal Purpose statement reads: *"…state tax withholding election, information on dependents, and to establish a Survivor Benefit Plan election."*

**Note 6 — 2656-series forms not fully verified.** Two further variants exist and were seen in official listings but their **full titles were not confirmed**:
- **DD Form 2656-5** — Reserve Component Survivor Benefit Plan (…) — title truncated in every accessible source. **UNVERIFIED.** (Edition seen: AUG 2011.)
- **DD Form 2656-10** — Survivor Benefit Plan (SBP)/Reserve Component (…) deemed election — title truncated. **UNVERIFIED.**
Do not publish either title until the form face is read.

**Note 7 — Source access constraints (methodology).** The DoD web estate blocks automated retrieval. `esd.whs.mil` (the authoritative DD-forms repository), `dfas.mil`, `militarypay.defense.gov`, `mortuary.af.mil`, `med.navy.mil`, `arpc.afrc.af.mil` and `marines.mil` all returned **HTTP 403** to every request, browser user-agent included. DoD form titles were therefore verified from official mirrors on `home.army.mil` (reading the rendered form face directly, the strongest available evidence) and cross-checked against VA/NARA/Military OneSource, all of which were reachable. `cem.va.gov` presented an incomplete TLS chain and required relaxed certificate verification to read.

**Note 8 — Nothing was found to be deprecated except eBenefits.** Every form in the table above is current as of the revision dates shown. No superseded form numbers were identified among the twelve submitted.
